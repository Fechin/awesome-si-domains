# TruthCheck.si — Follow the claim back to the evidence

![TruthCheck.si brand artwork](../assets/domains/truthcheck.si.webp)

**TruthCheck.si** makes a direct promise to editors, analysts, and product teams: investigate what supports an assertion. A useful service could compare source passages, methods, and contradictory accounts, then show exactly which part of a conclusion is well supported and which part still needs a human decision.

## Meaning and context

TruthCheck pairs an ambitious subject with a practical action. “Truth” points toward accuracy and reality; “check” suggests examination, comparison, and a process that can be repeated. The most convincing brand promise is therefore a visible review: identify the statement, inspect the source, record uncertainty, and explain the result. A buyer could apply that promise to publishing, research, AI answers, business communications, or education. The product can be confident about its process while remaining precise about what any particular body of evidence establishes.

## Ten directions a buyer could build

The following are proposed business opportunities. They show different ways to develop the name, with concrete audiences, workflows, and potential commercial models.

### 1. A publisher review desk

A publisher’s review desk could trace a draft’s important assertions to sources, compare contradictory accounts, and build a prioritized review queue around claims whose uncertainty would materially change the story. Publisher subscriptions could make factual review a maintained workflow rather than a final hurried step before an article or report is released.

### 2. AI citation and answer review

A generated-answer review tool could compare a generated explanation with the cited passages, test whether those passages support the actual claim, and isolate missing premises before the answer reaches a user. Team plans could connect questionable passages with prompts, retrieved material, model settings, and reviewer feedback, creating concrete examples for improvement.

### 3. Research evidence libraries

A research evidence library could connect a research question with methods, findings, limitations, and competing studies, organize a source-linked argument, and show which uncertainties still prevent a stronger conclusion. Professional subscriptions could help teams revisit a conclusion and see which parts rest on strong evidence or require more investigation.

### 4. Business presentation checking

A presentation-checking assistant could connect slides, source spreadsheets, supporting reports, and the intended message, detect inconsistent figures or overstated claims, and prepare specific corrections for the presenter to review. Business plans could retain approval records and source versions, making it easier to reuse accurate material without losing important qualifications.

### 5. Consumer information publishing

A consumer research publication could compare product claims, published specifications, test conditions, and user questions, explain where evidence differs, and help editors publish useful comparisons without manufacturing certainty. Editorial memberships and disclosed commerce could support readable guides showing the method, evidence, and unresolved questions relevant to a reader’s decision.

### 6. Classroom evidence literacy

An evidence-literacy tutor could connect a student’s belief with its sources and underlying assumptions, present an instructive counterexample, and guide a discussion about how evidence changes the strength of a claim. Course purchases and school licenses could teach repeatable reasoning skills through actual examples and instructor-reviewed activities.

### 7. Policy and public-interest research

A public-interest research workspace could reconcile policy statements, primary records, methodology, and affected groups’ experiences, compare interpretations, and make the reasoning behind a research conclusion available for scrutiny. Institutional plans could preserve a clear distinction between reported facts, organizational interpretation, and a position being advocated.

### 8. Marketing claim maintenance

A marketing claim register could connect marketing language with approved evidence, product changes, and publication locations, detect when a claim has become unsupported, and coordinate a reviewed correction across channels. Company subscriptions could route new drafts to the right expert and identify when an old claim needs reassessment because the underlying product or evidence changed.

### 9. Data consistency review

A data reconciliation workbench could trace conflicting figures through transformations, source versions, and business definitions, distinguish a calculation error from a difference in scope, and explain the reconciliation to decision-makers. Developer plans could create review queues with proposed corrections and source context, supporting the people responsible for deciding what the data should mean.

### 10. Transparent evaluation publishing

A transparent evaluation publication could connect benchmark claims with test design, raw examples, and uncertainty, investigate where an aggregate result hides important weaknesses, and prepare transparent findings with reproducible review paths. Professional plans could help organizations publish understandable evaluations of products or AI systems with the method available for scrutiny.

## A Super Intelligence product story

TruthCheck.si could express Super Intelligence as a partner in examining answers. One agent decomposes a draft into checkable claims, another retrieves permitted sources, and a reviewer sees the evidence beside each statement. The final output includes what is supported, what remains uncertain, and which corrections were approved.

## Why this name can carry a business

The compound describes an understandable action and creates a strong role for the product in a customer’s workflow. It can feel serious in a research platform and accessible in an educational service. A buyer has room for software, publishing, professional review services, and learning resources under the same identity.
