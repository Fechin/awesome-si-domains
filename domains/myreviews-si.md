# MyReviews.si — Make experience useful to the next decision

![MyReviews.si brand artwork](../assets/domains/myreviews.si.webp)

MyReviews.si joins personal ownership with the act of examining an experience or piece of work. A purchase journal or review workspace could connect an original goal, observed results and later reflections, giving the next decision better context. Customer feedback, professional development and creative critique offer other audiences; the common promise is a judgment grounded in something the reviewer can explain.

## What the name opens up

My Reviews can mean reviews I write, reviews about my business, or evaluations I want to revisit. Review itself can involve an assessment, a critique, a re-examination, or a study recap. That range supports consumer shopping, creator publishing, workplace reflection, education, and professional evaluation. My emphasizes ownership and perspective: a user should understand whose experience a statement represents. [Schema.org's Review type](https://schema.org/Review) offers a technical reference for describing an actual review and the item it concerns. The domain's value lies in its immediate first-person relationship with the person using it.

## Business opportunities

### 1. Personal purchase journal

A purchase reflection assistant could connect a person's original buying goal, usage observations and later experience, identifying what actually made a product useful. Shoppers would build a personal decision history rather than repeatedly trust recommendations that ignore their preferences. Premium consumer features would support a useful private memory even when nothing is published publicly.

### 2. Creator review collections

A creator review workspace could organize a reviewer's tests, comparison criteria and audience questions, identifying where an attractive conclusion lacks evidence. Creators would publish a coherent collection of explained judgments while preserving the difference between hands-on observation and inference. Creator subscriptions or paid publications would turn individual expertise into a coherent, searchable review resource.

### 3. Customer feedback workspaces

A customer feedback analyst could compare feedback across interactions, connect themes with service context and draft follow-up questions for ambiguous complaints. Businesses would understand the change customers need rather than treat sentiment alone as a complete explanation of the problem. Team subscriptions would connect feedback analysis with actual service follow-through and an understandable record of resolution.

### 4. Professional performance reflection

A professional reflection coach could connect a person's work examples, feedback and stated goals, prompting reflection on decisions and alternatives. Coaches would help the user identify a concrete development experiment instead of collapse a complicated professional experience into a rating. Individual subscriptions or employer editions would support a practical preparation tool with clear personal evidence.

### 5. Study review notebooks

A study review notebook could compare a learner's notes, attempted explanations and earlier mistakes, selecting retrieval questions that test understanding. Students would revisit the ideas they have not yet connected rather than repeatedly reread the material they already recognize. Consumer subscriptions or school licenses would use the re-examination sense for an education product.

### 6. Creative critique communities

A creative critique assistant could relate a creator's intended effect to the audience's responses, organize competing critiques and propose revisions that test a specific idea. Communities would discuss the work's objectives instead of collect disconnected preferences disguised as universal advice. Memberships, workshop fees, or professional groups could create a focused community built around useful critique.

### 7. Service experience journals

A service experience journal could connect a customer's appointments, communications and outcome observations, helping them prepare a clear account of an experience. Users would explain what happened and what resolution they seek without losing the sequence in a long message thread. Give households a record of contractors, travel services, lessons, or subscriptions they have actually used.

### 8. Business review response desks

A review-response drafting tool could connect a public review with permitted service records and company policies, propose a factual response and identify necessary internal follow-up. Businesses would respond to the underlying experience rather than publish a polished apology with no operational consequence. Team subscriptions would connect reputation work with genuine service improvement and a source-linked record.

### 9. Reviewer publishing studios

An editorial review coordinator could coordinate testing plans, draft comparisons, source checks and publication updates across a review studio. Editors would maintain a transparent relationship between evidence and recommendation as products, conditions and audience needs change. Creator subscriptions, paid publications, or disclosed commerce relationships could support a more useful review business.

### 10. Project retrospective libraries

A retrospective learning assistant could connect project decisions, changing assumptions and outcomes, compare explanations for what happened and propose a testable improvement. Teams would retain reusable lessons with context rather than store retrospective notes that nobody consults again. Organization subscriptions could make reviews a reusable operating resource connected to the next project.

## A Super Intelligence product vision

A MyReviews SI companion could help a user compare their own product experiences, retrieve the details behind an opinion, and prepare a clearly attributed review draft. A team edition might group submitted feedback into themes and link each observation to its source. Users would decide what becomes public. A first business could focus on a passionate buying community, such as photography equipment or home tools, with premium collection and comparison features. The intelligence story is useful memory and thoughtful synthesis, grounded in actual experiences and their supporting details.
