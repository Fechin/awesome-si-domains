# IAQ.si — Understand indoor conditions before deciding what to change

![IAQ.si brand artwork](../assets/domains/iaq.si.webp)

IAQ.si has an established technical reading: indoor air quality. That makes building monitoring, ventilation-related records and facilities coordination natural product territory. A reasoning layer could help property operators compare measurements with occupancy and maintenance context instead of interpret a reading alone. An answer-evaluation brand is a separate creative expansion of the letters, not another established meaning of the environmental term.

## Name roots and associations

IAQ conventionally stands for indoor air quality, a term used by the [US Environmental Protection Agency](https://www.epa.gov/air-quality/indoor-air-quality). That gives the name a clear connection to occupied buildings, ventilation, measurement, and the experiences of the people inside. A brand can serve property operators, equipment makers, contractors, or households. There is also room for an explicitly buyer-defined technology expansion such as Intelligence Answer Quality, connecting the same compact letters with evaluation and evidence. Those are separate product territories, not interchangeable technical definitions.

## Ten directions a buyer could build

### 1. Building air dashboards

A building-environment assistant could connect sensor histories, occupancy schedules, maintenance records and occupant observations, identifying competing explanations for an air-quality pattern. Facilities teams would receive prioritized investigations with the underlying evidence instead of a dashboard that only changes color. Building subscriptions could give facilities teams understandable records and follow-up workflows, while making clear that a single sensor reading does not establish an entire building’s safety.

### 2. HVAC contractor workspaces

A contractor preparation tool could assemble the relevant equipment history, reported symptoms and site conditions before a contractor's visit, suggesting questions and inspection sequences. Contractors would compare hypotheses and prepare an evidence-grounded service recommendation rather than begin every job from scratch. Per-company plans could connect measurements with the actual work performed and the questions that require an additional inspection or specialist review.

### 3. School facilities coordination

A school facilities planner could reconcile classroom schedules, monitoring observations, maintenance responsibilities and communication needs, proposing an intervention plan for staff review. School facilities teams would see which follow-up action belongs to whom and how progress should be documented. Organization subscriptions could prepare clear reports for decision makers without converting incomplete measurements into unsupported conclusions about individual health outcomes.

### 4. Sensor fleet management

A sensor diagnostic service could compare devices, calibration records and neighboring measurements, identifying data gaps or suspicious disagreement before drawing environmental conclusions. Building operators would distinguish a possible sensor problem from a pattern requiring investigation at the site. Manufacturer licensing could support reliable service delivery across deployments instead of leaving every customer to interpret raw data files independently.

### 5. Home environmental journals

A household environment journal could connect a family's recorded activities, comfort observations and sensor patterns, suggesting small observational experiments. Residents would learn which contextual questions to ask without mistaking a consumer dashboard for a definitive medical or engineering assessment. Consumer subscriptions could connect practical notes with source-linked educational resources and questions to raise with an appropriate professional.

### 6. Property handover evidence

A property evidence organizer could assemble a traceable record of monitoring periods, equipment service and unresolved environmental questions for a handover. Property teams would understand the limits of the evidence as well as the documents available for further review. Per-property fees could create a useful reference that remains understandable when a building passes from construction to day-to-day operation.

### 7. Workplace occupant communication

An occupant communication assistant could translate a facilities investigation into understandable updates, compare recurring occupant concerns and draft targeted follow-up questions. Organizations would explain what has been observed, what is being tested and what remains uncertain without flooding people with technical charts. Business plans could manage questions and updates while retaining a clear distinction between measured conditions and professional judgments.

### 8. Air-quality research collections

An environmental research workspace could compare study methods, measurement contexts and reported findings, organizing questions for a building-specific investigation. Researchers would inspect why evidence from one setting may or may not support a proposed inference in another. Institutional plans could make collection conditions visible and help collaborators interpret differences between datasets rather than combine incompatible readings indiscriminately.

### 9. Answer-quality evaluation for AI

Using Intelligent Answer Quality as a proposed brand expansion, an answer-evaluation agent could test answer evidence, reasoning consistency and tool execution against explicit rubrics. Product teams would identify a repairable failure mode rather than rely on a single opaque quality score. Developer subscriptions could connect each evaluation with the actual question, sources, model configuration, and human review.

### 10. Quality-focused agent operations

An agent-quality reviewer could review an agent's plan, retrieved evidence and completed actions, detecting where a seemingly successful task departed from the user's goal. Operators would compare corrective workflows and maintain a record of the decision behind each intervention. Enterprise plans could measure performance on defined tasks and retain correction histories rather than advertising an unexplained universal quality score.

## A Super Intelligence product story

IAQ.si could connect Super Intelligence with environments people can understand and improve. Building agents would summarize approved readings, identify missing context, prepare inspection tasks, and draft occupant updates for review. In the separate AI-evaluation direction, the same letters could emphasize inspectable evidence and quality of answers.

## Why this name can carry a business

The three-letter form is concise and technically recognizable in the building sector. It can support a serious professional platform or a clear consumer application. Its quality association also gives a buyer a considered expansion route into AI evaluation, provided the chosen interpretation is explained consistently.
