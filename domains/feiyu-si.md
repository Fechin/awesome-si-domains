# FeiYu.si — Move between worlds without losing the context

![FeiYu.si brand artwork](../assets/domains/feiyu.si.webp)

FeiYu.si is a pinyin spelling with several possible character choices; 飞鱼, flying fish, supplies one particularly vivid brand interpretation. Its movement between water and air suggests a companion that carries context across boundaries: research into deliverables, intentions into tasks, or unfamiliar places into a workable journey. Media, learning and multilingual business handoffs offer other coherent directions.

## Business opportunities

The strongest route depends on the buyer’s audience and the problem they want to solve. These directions develop different meanings of the name into practical product and service concepts.

### 1. Personal task companion

A personal assistant could connect an intention with relevant messages, tools and commitments, carrying context across the small tasks needed to achieve a larger outcome. Consumer subscriptions could emphasize visible progress and user-approved actions instead of requiring customers to manage every application separately.

### 2. Travel between unfamiliar places

Reconcile unfamiliar transport, local knowledge and a traveler's preferences, coordinating a journey that adapts when plans or circumstances change. Paid planning sessions or subscriptions could support journeys involving multiple languages, transport modes, and a mixture of booked and flexible activities.

### 3. Research-to-deliverable workspaces

A research workspace could connect evidence, specialist interpretations and a deliverable's purpose, turning an investigation into a useful presentation, brief or working plan. Team subscriptions could preserve source links and decision notes as work moves from initial exploration to a concrete client delivery.

### 4. Cross-application information transfer

An integration assistant could relate an application's records to the destination workflow, transferring the meaning and necessary context rather than merely copying fields. A software subscription could serve small teams that need information to travel reliably while retaining field mappings, permissions, and a history of changes.

### 5. Learning journeys

Connect a student's curiosity, existing understanding and practical challenges, guiding a journey across subjects toward a personally meaningful project. Education providers could license the platform, while tutors sell guided learning tracks that adapt to the student’s actual work rather than a fixed content sequence.

### 6. Creative media studio

A media assistant could coordinate research, visual storytelling and production specialists, translating an imaginative premise into coherent material across formats and languages. Subscription tools or project services could preserve the creator’s style while organizing the many small decisions involved in making a finished piece.

### 7. Marine observation tools

A marine assistant could relate observations, environmental context and research questions, helping field teams understand which patterns need additional sampling or expert attention. A field record could bring together an image, a location note and a research question, giving an expert the context needed to request a useful follow-up observation. Institutional plans could support review queues and exports for collaborators studying changing marine environments.

### 8. Playful consumer hardware character

Use a flying-fish character to connect physical interaction, personal preferences and useful device capabilities, giving an intelligent product a memorable everyday role. Hardware sales and optional software subscriptions could connect expressive design with practical household reminders and clearly controlled connected-device features.

### 9. Multilingual business handoffs

A business handoff assistant could connect language, relationship context and operational requirements, preparing shared understanding when a task moves between regions or teams. Business plans could connect human-reviewed translations with order context, terminology libraries, and task ownership through a cross-border transaction.

### 10. Adaptive career exploration

A career exploration assistant could relate a person's capabilities, changing interests and unfamiliar opportunities, designing practical experiments that make a possible transition easier to understand. Coaching subscriptions could help people move between disciplines, using evidence from completed work to decide which direction is worth pursuing next.

## A Super Intelligence product story

FeiYu.si could tell a Super Intelligence story about fluid movement between contexts. A research agent investigates a request, a planning agent proposes next steps, and execution tools prepare drafts or bookings. The interface keeps a single journey visible, so the user sees where information came from and what will happen next.

## Why this name can carry a business

Five letters and open vowels give Feiyu a light, character-friendly sound. Its optional flying-fish identity supports animation, a memorable mascot, and a sense of agility. The name can stretch across consumer assistance, learning, creative production, and technical coordination without becoming a narrowly descriptive category label.
