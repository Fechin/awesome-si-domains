# Choosing a domain for an AI business

A useful domain name tells a buyer what kind of relationship to expect: a capable partner, a precise instrument, a creative space, or a platform they control. Start with that promise, then compare names whose meanings support it. This guide shortlists domains from the collection by product direction; every linked article explores additional uses.

## What should an agent platform's name communicate?

An agent platform might be about personal control, specialist collaboration, or the infrastructure connecting tools. Those are different promises, even when the underlying software shares models and APIs.

| If the central promise is… | Explore | Why the distinction matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Your models, knowledge, and way of working** | [BYO.si](../domains/byo-si.md) | Bring Your Own and Build Your Own make customization part of the identity; Be Your Own adds an independent, personal reading. |
| **Several perspectives working together** | [NineFold.si](../domains/ninefold-si.md) | A name for coordinated specialists, scenario planning, or work examined from several angles. |
| **One place connecting distributed capability** | [OneNode.si](../domains/onenode-si.md) | The node metaphor fits compute, integration, knowledge networks, and an operator's control point. |
| **A partner that thinks alongside you** | [PeerBot.si](../domains/peerbot-si.md) | A peer suggests collaboration, critique, and reciprocal learning rather than a command-only interface. |
| **Understanding which agent is acting** | [BotID.si](../domains/botid-si.md) | Identity, delegated authority, and traceable actions give these compact letters a specific infrastructure role. |
| **Intelligence that takes useful action** | [Atua.si](../domains/atua-si.md) | Its Portuguese action-related reading offers a linguistic connection to execution, while its other cultural meanings support a richer character identity. |

Choose among these according to the buyer's experience, rather than treating every agent product as the same category. A personal workspace and an enterprise identity layer need different introductions.

## Reading, writing, and document reasoning

These names can share a knowledge engine while addressing quite different moments in a customer's work.

| Domain | A focused first product | Natural directions beyond it |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [ReadOn.si](../domains/readon-si.md) | A reading companion that follows questions across source passages | Accessible reading, learning paths, publishing, book communities, and turning insights into action |
| [InWords.si](../domains/inwords-si.md) | A workspace for expressing an idea clearly for its intended reader | Speech-to-text, translation, editorial work, research narratives, and personal stories |
| [Papered.si](../domains/papered-si.md) | An assistant that prepares a complete document packet and identifies missing evidence | Property applications, supplier onboarding, contracts, publishing, and business handovers |
| [AnnualReports.si](../domains/annualreports-si.md) | Source-linked comparisons across organizational reports | Longitudinal research, reporting preparation, impact narratives, and industry knowledge |
| [VDR.si](../domains/vdr-si.md) | A deal workspace that connects diligence questions with supporting documents | Visual document review, voyage records, evidence organization, and specialist research |

**ReadOn** begins with the reader; **InWords** with expression; **Papered** with preparation; **AnnualReports** with a particular source collection; **VDR** with a professional workspace. That difference gives each name a clearer audience and a more specific product story.

## Retrieval, transformation, or composition?

An audio company can help people find an existing recording, transform an asset, learn music, or create something new. The domain can make that distinction easier to grasp.

- **[AudioSearch.si](../domains/audiosearch-si.md)** places discovery first: finding speech, music, effects, or environmental sounds by meaning and context.
- **[AudioX.si](../domains/audiox-si.md)** offers a broader transformation identity for voice, restoration, production, and audio infrastructure.
- **[Solfa.si](../domains/solfa-si.md)** has a musical teaching foundation that suits ear training, harmony, voice practice, and composition.
- **[DrumLoop.si](../domains/drumloop-si.md)** introduces rhythm immediately, making sense for beat creation, loop discovery, adaptive practice, and performance tools.
- **[HearMe.si](../domains/hearme-si.md)** centers the speaker and the act of being understood, opening directions in voice workflows, interviews, communication, and personal histories.

A retrieval product may be easier to explain under AudioSearch; a rhythm-focused instrument under DrumLoop. A broader roadmap can still grow from that first promise.

## A descriptive name or a characterful brand?

**A descriptive compound** such as [TextReader.si](../domains/textreader-si.md), [HomeGuide.si](../domains/homeguide-si.md), or [JobReady.si](../domains/jobready-si.md) introduces a recognizable task. It suits a product whose first demonstration should explain the name.

**An evocative word** such as [Dipp.si](../domains/dipp-si.md), [Neep.si](../domains/neep-si.md), or [Rapt.si](../domains/rapt-si.md) gives a buyer more room to create a personality. The article explains the linguistic foundation and which product stories remain coherent with it: sampling and depth for Dipp, an approachable natural image for Neep, and absorbed attention for Rapt.

**A technical acronym** such as [DAQ.si](../domains/daq-si.md), [SCPI.si](../domains/scpi-si.md), or [LQG.si](../domains/lqg-si.md) can address a specialist audience directly. Start with the established meaning; introduce additional expansions as proposed brand readings, rather than presenting inventions as dictionary facts.

## Where does Super Intelligence fit?

Here, **SI gives a name a Super Intelligence direction**: a larger ambition for software that reasons across context, coordinates capabilities, and helps complete meaningful work. The word before the dot should still carry its own promise. A visitor should understand why the product is called ReadOn, RouteMapper, or HearMe before being asked to imagine its long-term capabilities.

A focused first release might retrieve evidence, compare a few alternatives, or prepare an action for review. The name can accommodate a more capable system later without pretending that the proposed business already exists.
