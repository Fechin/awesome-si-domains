# Why .si works as a Super Intelligence brand

**The word before the dot supplies the promise; SI gives it room to grow.** In this collection, `.si` is read as Super Intelligence: an identity for businesses building more capable ways to understand information, reason through choices, and coordinate useful action.

That identity becomes interesting when it connects to a specific human goal. A reader wants to understand a difficult report. A musician wants to develop a fragment into a composition. A service team needs to carry a customer's request through to a completed job. The name should help someone recognize that purpose.

## Let the name explain the work

| Name | What the word contributes | What a capable product could do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [ReadOn.si](../domains/readon-si.md) | A natural invitation to continue reading | Follow a question through passages, explain differences, and connect ideas to a reader's next step. |
| [Hues.si](../domains/hues-si.md) | Color, variation, mood, and visual character | Translate a creative brief into coherent palettes, material choices, and design alternatives. |
| [RouteMapper.si](../domains/routemapper-si.md) | The act of making a path understandable | Compare constraints, sequence work, and revise a journey or execution plan when conditions change. |
| [Cues.si](../domains/cues-si.md) | A signal that helps someone know what to do next | Recognize useful moments for a prompt across learning, conversation, performance, and everyday work. |
| [BYO.si](../domains/byo-si.md) | Bring Your Own, Build Your Own, and the creative reading Be Your Own | Connect a person's models, knowledge, tools, and preferences into a workspace they can shape. |
| [DAQ.si](../domains/daq-si.md) | An established abbreviation for data acquisition | Connect measurements with instrument context, experiment questions, and evidence a research team can inspect. |

These are distinct foundations. A precise term can introduce a specialist tool; a familiar phrase can make a complex capability approachable; an evocative word can create a character or philosophy.

## Build a story that survives the first feature

Consider **ReadOn**. A useful first product might help a reader ask questions about a document while keeping the relevant passages visible. Later, it could compare related documents, assemble a learning path, or help turn a conclusion into a written brief. Each extension grows from reading and understanding, so the identity remains coherent.

**BYO** tells a different story. Its value lies in ownership and configuration: bring a model, connect permitted knowledge, choose tools, and shape the assistant's role. A business could begin with a personal workspace and later support teams of specialist agents. The name carries the relationship between the customer and the system, rather than one fixed feature.

In both cases, the ambition is visible through the product's behavior. The business earns its explanation by showing what gets understood, which alternatives get considered, and how useful work follows.

## Give a broad name a focused starting point

Each domain article explores several independent business directions. Those are choices for a future owner. The most compelling launch story usually identifies one audience, one important problem, and a result someone can recognize.

For example, [AudioSearch.si](../domains/audiosearch-si.md) could begin with podcast researchers locating a particular claim in a large recording archive. Its wider possibilities include lecture retrieval, sound-effect discovery, music research, and an audio-search API. The shared foundation is finding meaningful sound; the first customer makes the initial product concrete.

## Separate meaning from possibility

The articles use dictionaries, standards, and technical references to support linguistic or professional meanings. Creative acronym expansions, new product concepts, and suggested slogans are editorial possibilities for a buyer. Keeping those roles clear makes a naming story more useful: a reader can identify what the word already means and what a new brand could make of it.

Superintelligence itself is a research concept. OpenAI's [discussion of superalignment](https://openai.com/index/introducing-superalignment/) addresses systems with capabilities beyond humans and the challenge of aligning them with human intent. It provides context for the ambition behind SI; the proposed businesses here begin with concrete workflows a future owner could develop.
