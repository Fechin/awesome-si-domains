# Make the right name yours

Choose a name for the Super Intelligence business you want to build. **[Email hedge.pike02@icloud.com](mailto:hedge.pike02@icloud.com?subject=Domain%20purchase%20enquiry)** with the domain and your preferred purchase route. Direct enquiries are welcome for an offer, a possible discount, or a bundle of complementary names.

The sale is for the domain name. Each article explores potential businesses for a future owner; software, an operating business, customers, and an audience are not included.

## Choose your purchase route

| Route | How to proceed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Direct enquiry** | [Email the owner](mailto:hedge.pike02@icloud.com?subject=Domain%20purchase%20enquiry) to discuss an offer, a possible discount, timing, and transfer arrangements. |
| **Escrow.com** | Agree the terms with the owner, then arrange the transaction using [Escrow.com's domain service](https://www.escrow.com/domains). |
| **Dynadot** | Follow the listing link in the domain article or search [Dynadot's marketplace](https://www.dynadot.com/market). |
| **Spaceship SellerHub** | Search the exact domain through [Spaceship](https://www.spaceship.com/) or request its current SellerHub purchase link. |
| **Sedo** | Search the exact name on [Sedo](https://sedo.com/) and review the available purchase or offer options. |
| **Afternic** | Search [Afternic](https://www.afternic.com/) for the domain and confirm current availability. |
| **Atom** | Search [Atom](https://www.atom.com/) or ask the owner for the domain's current purchase link. |

Availability and purchase options vary by domain and marketplace. If a search does not show a name, email the owner to arrange another route.

## Offers and flexible payment

Offers are welcome through the owner or a listing that supports negotiation. Six-month payment plans may be available where supported for the domain; review the platform's payment and transfer conditions before checkout.

## From enquiry to ownership

1. **Choose your name.** Include its exact spelling, your preferred route, and timing requirements in your email.
2. **Agree the terms.** Confirm the domain, consideration, currency, transfer method, and responsibility for transaction fees privately or through the marketplace.
3. **Complete the agreed transaction.** Follow the chosen service's payment and verification process. [Escrow.com's domain overview](https://www.escrow.com/domains) explains its payment and transfer sequence.
4. **Receive the domain.** Complete the registrar or account transfer, then configure the name for your project.

A product family might combine a consumer brand, a technical service, and a learning platform. If several names fit your plans, ask about them together.
