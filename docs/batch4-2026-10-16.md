### Meaning and naming construction

Topoi is an established plural of topos. Oxford documents its literary sense involving recurring subjects or ideas and traces topos to the Greek word for place. The Stacks Project separately uses topoi in mathematics and defines a topos, in its setting, as a category of sheaves on a site. These are genuine but distinct scholarly contexts; they should not be collapsed into a claim that a brand already provides mathematical reasoning.

[Oxford Learner's Dictionary: topos](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/topos). [Oxford Classical Dictionary: topos](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/61673/chapter-abstract/548206070). [The Stacks Project: Topoi](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00X9).

### Why the name stands out

Five letters give a substantial intellectual idea a compact address. The name can appeal to readers who know rhetoric or category theory, while a clear subtitle can explain the product to everyone else. Its strongest asset is the connection between organized ideas and places for knowledge. A specialist product can retain that depth, while an editorial or cultural brand can use a more approachable idea-atlas metaphor. Distinct positioning matters because Topoi already names a philosophy journal and an academic research network.
