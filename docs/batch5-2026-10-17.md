No particular game assets, model weights, datasets, software licences or physical goods are included in the domain sale. A future product would need its own supported formats and permissions.

An observed word, file format or independent same-name product does not establish trademark clearance, affiliation or a right to reuse another organization's identity. None of the suggested products is represented as already operating on this domain.
